- music player
rmpc (https://github.com/mierak/rmpc)

- disk viewer
dysk (https://github.com/Canop/dysk)

- socket statistics
ss
    Example:
    - ss -tuln | grep :<port_number>
        -t: Show TCP sockets.
        -u: Show UDP sockets.
        -l: Show listening sockets.
        -n: Do not resolve service names.
        Replace <port_number> with the specific port you want to check (e.g., 80, 443, 22).
